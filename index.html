Version 2.0 - Released October 6, 2026
v2.0 October 6, 2026
  • Semester picker removed: upload a file and click Process Files. No Semester column in the export.
  • Health Sciences pairing removed: Advising Office comes only from Assigned Staff advisor roles. The "Enrolled in any Clinical Course" categories remain in the Category Manager.
v1.6 October 6, 2026
  • Overview tab (renamed from Advising Office): summary cards, Students with Office Assignments, Advisor Assignments (no checkboxes), and a new Students by Major table. The No Office breakdown and Median Caseload sections were removed.
v1.5 October 6, 2026
  • Any Navigate export: header row detected automatically (line 1 or after the preamble); accepts Category/Categories and Student ID/EMPLID spellings.
  • No confirmation dialog: processing starts on click; Health Sciences logic unchanged.
  • Assigned Staff tab: tick which relationship types (Faculty Mentor, Peer Advisor, Librarian, Program Director, and any others in the file) to export as columns. Advisor relationships are built in and locked. Nothing ticked by default.
  • Category Manager: buckets reduced to New Student Cohorts, Transfer Out, Transfer In, Financial Aid, Service Indicators, Student Groups, Enrollment & Sessions, and Other.
  • Export: "Major" column renamed "Major with subplan".
v1.2 October 5, 2026
  • Removed: the Academic Departments tab and the "Student Academic Department" / "Student Academic Department_sub" export columns. Tabs are now Advising Office and Category Manager.
v1.1 October 2, 2026
  • Dashboard fix: The per-office rows under "Multiple Advisors (same office)" were showing Health Sciences-only students. They now show the actual multi-advisor offices. Headline counts were unaffected.
v1.0 October 2, 2026
  • Initial release. A streamlined version of the Navigate Caseload Generator (forked from v3.9.20). Upload the Navigate enrollment export, pick the semester, choose categories, and export.
  • Required file only: No optional lookup files, so the export omits Staff ID, Mentor ID, Peer ID, Faculty department columns, and Admit Type.
  • Category search: Type to filter categories; use Select matching / Clear matching for the visible rows. Nothing is selected by default.
